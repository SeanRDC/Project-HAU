# 2026-10-14: the Immaculate Heart Gymnasium, outside and in

**Who:** Sean, with Claude Code.

## What was asked
Sean added 21 photos in `assets/ih_gym immaculate heart/`. Update the front view, the side view and the interior of the IH Gym, beside SJH: a big gym where events are held. The player must see the real front and be able to go round to the side. The building has three entrances, one on the left side and two on the front. The player must be able to go from the first floor up to the second. It has to be detailed, because an event will be held here soon.

## Questions asked, and Sean's answers
* **Where does the front's right door (the glass door under the seal and HOLY ANGEL UNIVERSITY GYM) lead?** Straight onto the court.
* **Where does the corridor from the front's left door come out?** In the lobby inside the side entrance.
* **Is the stage at the end farthest from the side entrance?** Yes.

## Decisions
* **Which side is which.** On the aerial photo and in OSM the gym is long north-west to south-east (74 m by 44 m). Its front is the long south-west side on the parking lot, the side the existing Gym lane scene already looks at. Its left side is the north-west gable end, facing the covered court across a 6 m lane. That is the side entrance "beside the other court". The annex juts out in front of the far end of the front; it is the dark ribbed shed with the seal painted on it in the photo of the right entrance.
* **Seven scenes:**

  | Scene | What it shows |
  | --- | --- |
  | Front (`gym`, redrawn) | The front of the gym |
  | Side (`gymside`, new) | The lane to the side entrance |
  | Lobby (`gymlobby`) | Inside the side entrance |
  | Corridor (`gymcorridor`) | In from the front's left door, with the equipment room and the stairs |
  | Court (`gymhall`) | The court floor |
  | Stage (`gymstage`) | The stage at the south-east end |
  | Second floor (`gymupper`) | The bleachers |

  Inside, the camera looks the same way as outside wherever it can, so left and right stay the same: the court and the bleachers look north-east across the court, with the side entrance end on the left and the stage end on the right. The lobby and the corridor look south-east like the side scene.
* **How you get round:**
  * Front left door → corridor. From there the stairs go up to the second floor, and the far end of the corridor goes into the lobby.
  * Front right door → the court, near the stage end.
  * Side entrance → lobby. Its gates open onto the court.
  * Court → the stage.
  * Stage → stairs up to the bleachers.
  * Bleachers: the stairs at the far end go back down to the corridor.
* **Going up and down stairs is a cutscene.** Up from the corridor you climb the flight into the depth of the scene, seen from behind. Coming down you walk out of it towards the camera.
* **The equipment room is on the camera's side of the corridor** (left as you walk in, as in the photo), so it is a "look back" hotspot, and its diagonal grille and shelves of balls are drawn in the foreground.
* **Inside is not to the 9 px a metre scale**, like the DJN lobby: the court is drawn about 16 px a metre so it fills the screen.
* **For the event:** the stage scene exports `STAGE` (its left, right, middle, deck and floor rows) for anything an event wants to put on it. The court and the stage have room on the floor for crowds.
