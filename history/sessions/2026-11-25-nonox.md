# Nonox

**Who:** Jorel, with Claude Code.

## What was asked

Add Nonox, a 6-month-old blue-grey Persian kitten based on a real cat, from a handoff
(`NONOX_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`, a 26 px sprite sheet and mockups, now in
`assets/drafts/characters/critters/nonox/`). He lives in one building, wanders it, roams out
now and then and comes back, walks up to the student to say Meow!, and can be petted with E
("Hawakan si Nonox"). The plan is in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-25-nonox.md`.

## Decisions

- **One more cat in `critters.gd`, not a new system.** The handoff assumed prefabs, a navmesh
  and an interaction component. Here the cats already have the sprite setup, lighting and
  shadows, the "Hawakan si ..." prompt, the bubble, meow and purr. Nonox is an entry in `cats`
  with his own state machine (`_update_nonox`) and a config dict (`NONOX`).
- **Home: St. Joseph Hall's raised walkway** (Jorel's choice), not APS or the Main Building.
- **Size: ~0.68 of Mingming's height** (Jorel: 0.65-0.7). Measured, Mingming sits 31 px and
  Nonox 24 px, so at the shared density he would be 0.77. He is drawn at 0.88 of `DENSITY`,
  the one sprite not at the characters' density (in the style guide).
- **Speed 1.44 m/s** (0.6 of the student's walk, from the handoff; approved), more than
  twice the other cats' 0.6 m/s stroll. The walk frame advances every 0.18 m (8 fps at that
  speed), so his feet stay planted.
- **No navmesh, so trips are fixed routes of straight legs** (`_nonox_routes()`, in SJH's
  frame): down the lobby steps to the kerb, along the brown sidewalk east or west, and
  through the west passage down to the yard. `--test` checks every leg both ways. His wander
  targets must have a straight line back to home (or, on a trip, to where he stopped), so a
  trip can always start and end cleanly.
- **He never waits off-screen.** The other cats freeze when off-screen (performance); Nonox
  is excluded so he can come home while you are elsewhere. One cat's collision queries a frame.
- **The free cam (F2) counts as talking** for the critters, so no cat comes to a student
  left standing while the camera flies.
- **The sheet's empty bottom row is dropped** (`split.mjs`): every cell had one, so he would
  float ~2 cm.
- **The real cat's photo was not committed** (the repo is public); it stays with the handoff.
- **Not built** (optional or off in the handoff): following the student after 10 pets,
  saving his place, roaming more in the afternoon, and a startled run (he has no gallop frames).

## What changed

- `critters.gd`: the Nonox section (`NONOX`, `_nonox_routes`, `_update_nonox`, `_pet_nonox`,
  trips, approach, greet, sulk, petted, linger); `pet()` gives him a 3 s cooldown; `_show()`
  uses each sprite's own pixel size; `_walk()` takes a cat's own stride.
- `aps.gd`: `--test` checks `nonox_home`, `nonox_roams`, `nonox_greets`, `nonox_petted`; F1
  "Nonox, SJH walkway"; the free cam passes `talking`.
- `sprites/critters/nonox_*.png`, made by `assets/drafts/characters/critters/nonox/split.mjs`.
- `docs/godot-art-style.md`: Nonox in the cats paragraph.

## Left unfinished / known problems

- The greet and petting were checked by `--test` and the home by a capture, not played
  through by hand.
- On a trip he can still be petted or greet you; after a pet mid-trip he carries on along the
  route. If a route leg ever becomes blocked (a new prop on SJH's front), he hops to that leg's
  end rather than getting stuck, and the check names the leg.
- Godot on macOS: `tools/run-aps-godot.cmd` is Windows only. On a Mac, put the 4.7.2 macOS
  build in `builds/godot-tools/4.7.2/` and run
  `Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --path src/drafts/aps-godot --headless -- --test`.
