# APS and its canteen, redrawn from the walk from the chapel lawn

Date: 2026-10-14

**Who:** Mikko (H1enZ).

## What was asked
> I have new references for APS BUILDING AND APS CANTEEN SO U CAN COPY IT ACCURATELY. Imagine you are walking from chapel lawn to APS BUILDING AND CANTEEN

Twenty new photos, taken in order walking from the chapel lawn past PGN, round the corner and along the front of APS to the canteen. They are in `assets/APS WALK FROM CHAPEL LAWN/`, numbered 01 to 20 in the order they were taken.

## What the photos showed that the scene had wrong
* **The order along the road.** Walking from the lawn: the long four-floor wing, the deep canopy over the main entrance, the tall block with curved balconies, a plain white stair tower, then the canteen block. The scene had the canopy at the lawn end and the louvres at the far end; it is the other way round (the older photo set agrees, see photos 5 to 8 there).
* **Three buildings, not one.** The canteen was drawn as one six-floor block with the stair tower on its left. In fact the curved-balcony block, the stair tower and the canteen block are three different shapes, in that order: the tower is between the balcony block and the canteen, not before them.
* **The canteen block** is cream, five floors, with a deep white box with rounded corners under every window, not curved balconies. Its awning is blue-grey sheeting from outside.
* **The canopy** has thick stone pillars with tall cream poster panels fixed to them, a grey rain-streaked underside, and white tiles with red-brown diamonds underfoot. Behind it: brown doors, the cashier's window with the TV above, whiteboards on green stands, a grilled window and a cash machine.
* **In front of the tall block:** a potted ficus, the bronze bust on a black pedestal, the big campus map on two posts, a mesh cage of plastic bottles, then the umbrella patio on herringbone brick, which runs on past the tower and the canteen.
* **The twin-lantern posts** are rusty bronze, not black.
