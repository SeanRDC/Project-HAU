## Paeng's cutscene art

- Mclaren's two pictures of Paeng (at school in a black hoodie; ten years later in a black
  polo) are `cutscene-art/paeng-source.png` and `paeng-older-source.png`, cropped from
  1086 x 1448 to 2:3 and scaled to 1024 x 1536 like the others' sources.
- PixelLab answered 402 (0 of 5000 generations, no credits), so `paeng.png` and
  `paeng-older.png` were made locally at 256 x 384 with the new `tools/pixelart-local.py`.
- **Shrinking by averaging was wrong** (the first two tries: median then box, 40 then 34
  colours, lone pixels cleaned). Where dark hair met the light wall the average was an
  in-between colour that snapped to skin orange or strap navy, so the outline was lined
  with specks and thin strands broke; Mclaren saw it ("many wrong / dead pixels").
- **What works:** never average. Palette from the flat parts only, close shades joined;
  each pixel takes the most common palette colour among the flat pixels of its cell; a
  line thinner than a cell (the mouth) is kept when it is darker or lighter than every
  flat colour for a cell and a half round it (compared closer in, every outline grew
  ragged); tiny patches left between a much lighter and a much darker neighbour are
  removed. Result: 13 and 9 colours, about 270 lone pixels each (the PixelLab ones have
  3,000 to 7,000).
- **Matched to the cast's style** (Mclaren: "not perfect like the others"). Measured
  against the six: the cast is warm sepia (wall 217,205,188; darks 53,36,29 to 70,46,36;
  skin 206,140,93), has no eye whites and soft brown lines; Paeng's sources are pink and
  purple-tinted (wall 237,217,206; darks 65,44,51) with white eyes and near-black lines.
  New `tools/cutscene-grade.py` moves each palette colour onto ramps of the cast's own
  colours (neutral, skin, blue) and makes the eye whites skin. No pixel moves.
- **The older Paeng's thin lines** (Mclaren: "the old one is not yet refined"). His source
  has lines two or three pixels wide, under one cell, so the per-cell test dropped them
  wherever they straddled two cells: the face and ear outlines were dashed, the mouth
  broken, the irises gone. `pixelart-local.py` now finds thin lines in the picture first
  (a closing and an opening of the brightness, 9 px) and draws each one pixel wide in
  the cell that holds most of it. `cutscene-grade.py` makes the iris the cast's brown
  (121,57,32 and 93,44,26) and softens the hair highlight. Both pictures were remade
  this way.
- **Then the outlines were too thick** (Mclaren noticed). Counted: two-pixel dark lines
  went from 354 to 494 on the older picture when line tracing came in. A drawn line is
  about 6 px of 1024, a cell and a half, and "a cell that is 45% line is line" made
  both cells line. That shortcut is gone: a cell is line only when it holds more of the
  line than the cells either side, so every traced line is one pixel (one-pixel lines
  now 505, two-pixel 319). Light specks of wall seen past an ear become skin
  (`cutscene-grade.py`).
- Still different, from the sources themselves: Paeng is drawn with harder outlines,
  flatter fills (14 and 9 colours; the cast has 36 to 50) and a smaller head.
- PixelLab's subscription is expired (balance: 0 generations, 0 USD), not just used up.
- Still to redo with `pixellab-convert.mjs --size=256x384 --strength=150` when credits
  come back, so all eight are made the same way.
- `cast-draft.png` rebuilt with a fourth column for Paeng.
- The older Paeng's polo has a real brand's laurel logo; swap it before release.

## The story, revised

Written into `opening/STORY.md` as "The story as revised on 2026-11-14 (read this
first)", above the 2026-10-27 text, which is not yet rewritten to match. In short:

- The opening becomes police sworn statements (idea from *Kuchi ni Kansuru Anketo*).
- Ysha is a student assistant with unsupportive parents; she goes to Consuelo, Macabebe
  on November 11 for one calm day by the sea, in a typhoon.
- Paeng, who likes her, follows to bring her home, they argue, and he shoves her over the
  seawall without meaning to. Police: self-harm. Lance: the typhoon. Pat saw a man who
  looked like Paeng pass her grandparents' house and never said. Paeng is never found out.
- Lance learns it in the second life by watching Paeng, then by walking the road himself.
- The game ends on a phone: "Call" or "Put it down". If he calls, Paeng says one line,
  Mclaren's: "Thank you, pre. Ililigtas mo ako." Cut to black.
- No dengue, no fountain promise, no 11:11, no rumour post by Paeng.
- Tried on the way and dropped: the Abacan River, a promised trip she took alone, Paeng
  as her old blockmate who turned her away from a group project.
- Claude's proposals Mclaren has not confirmed are listed in STORY.md (Lance's guilt, the
  4:47 PM call, how the shove happens).
- Consuelo was checked against Mclaren's nine photos and a drone video (frames pulled
  with yt-dlp and ffmpeg into the session's temp folder; none are in the repo).

## Mascot (draft)

Mclaren asked for a guide mascot "like Takopi". Drafted the project's own, not a copy: a
doodle Ysha drew in ballpen on a handout, come to life, with a paper boat for a hat and
hands that don't match. Source and pictures in `assets/drafts/characters/mascot/`. Proposed
role: it lives in her sketchbook, takes Lance back, guides the player, can't lie and
doesn't understand sadness; the sketchbook was in her bag at Consuelo, so it heard what
happened there without understanding it. Nothing is decided; no name yet.

It also has a 3D model in a T-pose with a 13-bone skeleton: `tools/blender/mascot.py`
writes `models/mascot.glb` and `assets/drafts/3d/mascot.blend` (Blender 5.2 is at
`C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\`). Not wired into `actor.gd`; no
animations. A first run rendered its previews to `C:uilds\mascot-3d` (Blender resolves a
relative render path from the drive root); the script now makes the path absolute, and
that stray folder is left for Mclaren to delete.
