# The night sky

**Who:** Mikko, with Claude Code.

## What was asked

Improve the night sky's stars. Mikko looks at the sky in the F2 free cam, and asked for
more, varied stars, a gentle twinkle, a Milky Way band and a moon.

## Decisions

- **Where it shows:** the game's orthographic views look down at the campus, so they
  almost never show sky (not even close up); the free cam is where the sky is seen.
  There the sky is now laid out **by direction** (`free_look`, set when F2 starts and
  cleared when it ends): stars on a cube of 512 cells a face, so they stay put as the
  camera turns, instead of sticking to the screen. The game views keep a screen layout
  with the same improvements (2-px cells, the moon high on the right).
- **Stars:** about 1 cell in 100 (more in the Milky Way), brightness weighted towards
  faint, three tints (blue-white, white, warm), the brightest (1 in ~1400) with a
  one-cell pixel cross; about half twinkle slowly, each at its own pace. They thin out
  near the moon and fade towards the horizon. A first pass at 1 in 70 looked snowy.
- **Milky Way:** a faint patchy haze round a great circle, with more stars in it.
- **Moon:** drawn about four times its real size so it reads, pale with soft dark
  patches and a darker limb, a two-part glow; placed where the night light comes from
  (`moon_dir`, set with the sun in `_aim_sun`), so it matches the shadows.
- **Rain** (`overcast`, from atmosphere.gd) hides the stars, the Milky Way and the moon.
- The old hash used `sin()`, which on the GPU loses precision at large cell numbers and
  lined the stars up in streaks (only the dense band showed any); the hashes are now
  sine-free.
- `--free-cam=<yaw>,<pitch>` starts a capture in the free cam, for checking the sky.

## What changed

- `shaders/sky.gdshader`: the night sky (`night_by_direction`, `night_on_screen`,
  `star`, `moon_face`, `sky_cell`, value noise), `overcast`, `free_look`, `moon_dir`.
- `aps.gd`: `free_look` on F2 and off in `_leave_free_cam`; `moon_dir` in `_aim_sun`;
  `--free-cam` (`_look_freely`).
- `atmosphere.gd`: `overcast` with the rain.
- `docs/godot-art-style.md`: the sky shader row, the preview flags.

## Checks

`tools\run-aps-godot.cmd --headless -- --test`: all true. `npm run check` and
`npm run build` pass. Free cam captures at 21:00 looking at the moon and the Milky Way
and away from them checked. The twinkle moves, so stills can't show it: Mikko to check.
