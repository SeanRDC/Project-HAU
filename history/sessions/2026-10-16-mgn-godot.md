# MGN in Godot, on its plot (outside only)

**Who:** Jorel, with Claude Code (branch `mgn-on-plan`).

## What was asked

Build the Mamerto G. Nepomuceno Building (MGN, plan id `mgn`, map no. 14) on its plan
plot in Godot, outside only, from the MGN photos, checked against an annotated aerial
picture of the campus. No interior.

## What the attached picture showed (it is not in the repo)

Jorel's aerial picture with each building outlined in colour:

- **MGN (pink)** is at the bottom-left, west of the chapel lawn. Its long side runs north
  to south, at right angles to **St. Joseph Hall (blue)**, which runs west to east along
  the bottom. MGN's north end is level with the chapel's south end. Its south end widens
  and comes nearly up to SJH's west end.
- Its **east side faces the campus road** (the west road, grey), across from the park
  (brown) and the chapel (yellow). APS is at the top and Sacred Heart, the covered court
  and the IH Gym are on the right.
- The blue "L" just north of SJH's west end is the Mary statue's island; the red dashes
  are the two rows of parked cars.

**It agrees with the plot** in `CampusPlan.PLOTS["mgn"]` / `plan.ts`: same place, same
shape (narrow long block, wider south end, a bump towards the road at the north) and the
same turn (about 1.4° off the grid). Scaled from SJH's 96 m, the picture's MGN is about
67 × 12–26 m; the plot is 69 × 15–29 m. Nothing was moved; `plan.ts` is untouched.

The photos agree too: photos 01, 03 and 05 look west from the road by SJH, with SJH's west
end on the left (south), Mary in front, the corridor block in the middle and the box-window
wing on the right (north). The PGN walkway photo shows MGN's narrow north end.

## Decisions (Jorel's answers)

- **5 storeys**: an open ground floor (3.6 m) and four corridor floors (3.4 m), roof
  about 17 m, the tanks above.
- **Walk in up to the turnstile**: the west road's sidewalk and the paved entry under the
  box-window wing are walkable; the turnstile, desk, bins, columns, stair and garden are solid.
- **South end**: the south wing and its service block with a roof tank; no skybridge.
- **Camera**: on the west road beside MGN the camera turns right round to look west at its
  front, the chapel lawn cut away behind it; at the corner it turns a quarter more into
  SJH's view. Before this, the chapel view's camera stood inside MGN's plot.

## What changed

- `areas/mgn.gd` (new): MGN under its own pivot ("MGN", local +x north, +z east). The plot
  outline is straight in that frame, so every part sits on its corners: the long block
  with open corridors (thick white balcony bands, steel rails, columns, darker corridor
  walls with doors, windows and tube lights, "MGN 0x" room plates on the ground floor); the
  box-window wing with its porch (columns, glass doors, turnstile, guard's desk, the green
  "MGN 09" bin and a yellow one, the open stair behind its brown grille, a porch light); the
  north block with pairs of windows on its north face; the south wing with the service
  block and tank; the raised garden behind a white kerb wall with rocks, three shade trees,
  short palms and shrubs. Static `local()` / `world()` / `inside()` / `in_entry()` /
  `reach()` / `view_for()` / `walkable()` / `blocked()` / `floor_height()`.
- `world.gd`: MGN built next to SJH; its entry walkable, its body and garden blocked, the
  porch floor 0.15; the west clamp is now x −68.
- `aps.gd`: the MGN zone (`mgn`, three quarter turns), the chapel zone yields to it, its
  focus (the corridor front, the whole frontage when wide, the student's depth close up),
  the near-plane cut east of `Mgn.CUT_X`, `--view=mgn`, and 9 new checks (85 in all):
  `mgn_built`, `mgn_faces_road`, `mgn_solid_and_garden`, `mgn_entry_to_turnstile`,
  `west_road_beside_mgn`, `mgn_walk_in`, `walk_past_mgn`, `mgn_view_faces_front`,
  `mgn_close_shows_student`.
- `tools/export-godot-plan.mjs` (`mgn` built) and the regenerated `campus_plan.gd`.
- `tools/capture-aps-godot.sh` (5 MGN shots), `docs/godot-art-style.md` (file, MGN zone,
  porch light, macOS), `src/drafts/aps-godot/README.md`.

- After merging `main` (dev fast travel, the Mary and SJH polish): MGN's two spots,
  "MGN" (the road in front) and "MGN entrance" (the porch), added to `travel_places()`.

## Validation

- Headless `--test` with Godot 4.7.2 on macOS: all 85 checks true, `checks_pass` true.
- Captures at morning, noon, afternoon and night, wide (96 m), whole building (56 m), the
  entrance close up (18 m) and the road corner, compared with the photos. Fixed from them:
  the 56 m framing missed the south end (now centred on the frontage); the corridors read
  as a flat wall (their walls and soffits are now darker, as in the photos); the tank was
  too big and dark; the cut sliced SJH's terracotta end block (the cut moved 0.7 m east).
- `npm run check` and `npm run build` pass.

## Guesses ("approximate, from the photos")

- Storey heights (3.6 m ground, 3.4 m above) and so the 17 m roof.
- 10 corridor bays of 3.6 m; which bays have doors or windows; which rooms are lit.
- Corridor depth (2.6 m) and the balcony band (1 m with a rail on top).
- The box windows' size and place (south half of the wing's face), the narrow window
  beside them, the ledges.
- The porch: the turnstile's position and posts, the guard's desk, the doors, the bins'
  places, and the stair rising south to a landing at 3 m.
- The north block's window pairs and the east-face slits; the south wing's windows,
  service block and doorway; both tanks' size.
- The garden: tree, palm, shrub and rock positions; the kerb wall's height (0.55 m).
- The back (west) faces are not in any photo: rows of plain windows.

## Left unfinished

- No interior and no upper corridors to walk; the stair is solid.
- The lane between MGN's south half and SJH (in the closeup photo) and the skybridge are
  not built; the student only sees the south half from the road.
- The covered walk with the pergola along MGN's north end (PGN walkway photo) is off the
  plot and not built.
- The east face is in shade in the afternoon (it faces east), so it reads greyer than the
  midday photos.
- Like the SJH view, wide framings show an empty band at the bottom where the cut removes
  the near lawn. At the frame's left edge the cut can still clip a sliver of SJH's end.
