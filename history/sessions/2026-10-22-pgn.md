# PGN

**Who:** Mikko, with Claude Code.

## What was asked

"PGN next", from the PGN photos (PGN MIDDLE, PGN LEFT VIEW, PGN LEFT ENTRANCE, PGN RIGHT
SIDE VIEW ON APS and its close-up), "make it simple to understand". Asked, Mikko said:
it is an L like the plan, a ground floor + 9 floors + a box on top, and build the lane.

## What was built

- `areas/pgn.gd`, in world coordinates:
  - An L: the long block along the boulevard (face at z 49.2) and the wing going north
    beside APS on the west road.
  - A dark grey ground floor set back under the first band, with windows and grey columns.
  - Nine floors: each a deep white band with rounded outside corners, over a row of blue
    windows in white frames, grey louvres angled between the bands.
  - On the boulevard face: the tall white louvred stair screen, the name in gold
    ("PETER G. NEPOMUCENO" / "PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT", the words readable in the
    photos), the orange strip over the covered walkway, glass doors with yellow-edged steps.
  - The box on the roof with its row of small vents, under a white slab.
  - The west end: the blue glass stair tower, the dark louvred corner with the name in
    steel, and the paver lane with potted trees north to a small courtyard by STL's plot.
- The boulevard's north planter opens onto the lane (`MiddleRoad.PGN_LANE`), tiled through.
- Camera: in the lane it looks north up it (`Pgn.lane_for`), as in the photo, instead of
  turning east as the chapel's view did there. Facing south across the boulevard, the cut
  is now just in front of PGN's bands (`MIDDLE_CUT_Z` 50.4, was 30), so the building is
  not in the way.
- The plot is marked built.

- At its north end, the dark drive-through passage (`PASSAGE`) with yellow-edged kerbs
  and a concrete apron, and a lawn strip with a curved kerb, a rounded clipped hedge and
  the NO PARKING board.

  a quarter back from north, not three on, so coming from APS's road it turns the short way.
