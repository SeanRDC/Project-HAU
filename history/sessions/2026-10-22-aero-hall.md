# Aero Hall

**Who:** Mikko, with Claude Code.

## What was asked

"The warehouse between SJH and the gym", from IMG_1519.JPG. Asked, Mikko said it is Aero
Hall (the plan's "annex" plot, which wraps round the gym) and to build the yard too.

## What was built

- `areas/aero.gd`, in world coordinates, on the plot's front part, in front of the gym's
  south end just past its GYM doorway:
  - One storey of navy corrugated walls on a white base, under dark gable roofs with
    black fascias.
  - A taller gabled front block at the north end, its gable facing the gym's doorway, with
    the round HAU seal on its side and a door.
  - Three windows with white aircon units, the NO PARKING / MOTORCYCLE PARKING AT SJH
    BASEMENT sign, and a white planter with shrubs.
  - On the concrete in front: the green MOTORCYCLE PARKING board, the NO PARKING stand and
    a blank board.
  - The yard towards SJH, behind a green-painted wall with stains: a grey wall at the
    campus edge with a gate, a skip, a blue bin, cut branches, a small steel-framed
    shelter, and a pole with a street lamp.
- The concrete between SJH and the hall is walkable, starting at x 38.8, which leaves
  room for SJH moving 2 m east (sjh-mgn-spacing).
- Camera: facing the gym's front from out here it would stand inside SJH, so past the
  road's end it cuts away everything west of x 38.6.
- The annex plot is marked built. Its strips behind the gym are left bare: not drawn and
  not walkable.
