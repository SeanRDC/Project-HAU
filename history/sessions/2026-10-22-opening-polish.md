# The opening, polished

**Who:** Mclaren (Aistwobig), with Claude Code.

## What was asked

Make the opening less predictable and less cheesy, push the story further, and improve
the sky.

## Decisions

- **Page 10 is gone.** The opening ends on page 9 and the title; waking in the classroom
  becomes the game's first scene instead.
- **Page 8 is only facts:** "Nobyembre 11. / 11:11 PM. / 1 missed call." The "sana" list
  gave the ending away too early.
- **Page 9 is one line in the second person:** "Labindalawang taon ka nang naghihintay ng
  'bukas.'" It echoes his "bukas na lang" on the call and her voicemail.
- **Page 2's clouds** are puffy manga cumulus with screentone shading underneath.
- **The story** (`opening/STORY.md`): the voicemail is only about returning his Ethics
  notes; how the story should feel (ordinary days first, a playable 2038 prologue, small
  kindnesses that restore the photo, time for grief); the reveals underneath Ysha's story
  (the rumour started with Lance, her sketchbook is the opening, her doodle self, the
  smiling mother, her hidden account).
- Photo backgrounds: a filter can't make true manga backgrounds; the plan is hand-edited
  pictures (Clip Studio's line and tone conversion, then cleanup) dropped into
  `opening/panels/`.

## Left unfinished

- Pictures to draw: `alaala` (page 1), `mata` (page 9); the edited fountain background.
- The 2038 prologue and the classroom start are story only, not built.
