# St. Joseph Hall in Godot

**Who:** Mikko, with Claude Code.

## What was asked

Build St. Joseph Hall from the SJH photos (front view, left and right sides).

## Decisions (Mikko chose each one)

- **The camera turns to face it.** SJH's front faces north, across the parking
  toward the chapel, so on the south road, the parking and the walkway the camera turns
  right round (−180°). Key 9 jumps there. The south road (and so the whole road loop)
  is now walkable.
- **Scope: all four parts.**
  - **The exterior:** nine storeys on its plan plot, 96 × 19 m. It has white balcony
    floors with brown-posted railings, teal glass panels in staggered bays, brown
    pilasters and a glazed top floor in terracotta frames. At both ends stand a
    terracotta block and a set-back cream stair tower. The centre tower has a
    silver-clad frame, dark glass bands, the ST. JOSEPH HALL sign, a louvred entrance,
    a canopy and steps.
  - **The Mary statue garden:** a rounded grass island with a yellow-edged kerb at the
    building's right (west) end as seen from the front, by the cream stair tower and
    the road's turn (Mikko corrected it from the east end), with shrubs and bamboo.
  - **The parking:** north of the south road, with seeded rows of simple parked cars
    (the first cars in the scene).
  - **The ground-floor walk-in:** a covered walkway under an awning on slanted struts,
    behind a railing with gaps. Inside are room doors, windows and notice boards.
    Walking in uses the hallway's cutaway.
- **No inside description was given**, so the walkway's inner wall is built from what
  the photos show (doors, dark windows, notice boards, fire alarms). Correct it if the
  real one differs.
- **Photos:** all six copied into `assets/SJH BUILDING/` under their Downloads names
  (the numbered copies from before stay).

## Correction from Mikko's map

Mikko marked the layout on the aerial photo: SJH sits further back from the road than its
OSM outline, with **two rows of parked cars** (one on the chapel side of the south road,
one in front of SJH) and the **Mary statue at SJH's west end, in front of it**, beside the
front row. So SJH is set back 10 m (`SETBACK`), a front car row faces the road (with a gap
at the entrance steps), and the statue's island moved there. This is the one building
placed off its plan outline, on purpose.

## What changed

- New `areas/sjh.gd`, built under its own pivot (it is turned 1.9° to its plot edge).
- `world.gd`: the road loop is all walkable; SJH collision, floors, walkway and
  parking are added; grass stays off the parking.
- `aps.gd`: the SJH camera zone (−180°), framing 9 and the spot to start there. The
  chapel and gym zones yield to it. The cutaway is generalised to any corridor
  direction. Facing SJH, the camera also cuts away everything north of the parking.
  New checks.
- `areas/chapel.gd`: two trees moved out of the new parking.
- `tools/export-godot-plan.mjs` (`sjh` built) and the regenerated `campus_plan.gd`.
- Docs: the style guide (file, framing 9, SJH zone, generalised cutaway), the Godot
  README and `CONTEXT.md`.
