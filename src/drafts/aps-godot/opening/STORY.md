# Project HAU: story draft

The story the opening sets up. Draft, kept in step with the team's "Project HAU: Story
Draft" doc. The opening itself, page by page, is in [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md).

**Trigger warning:** this story deals with depression, suicide, grief and guilt. Nothing is
shown on screen, but the subject is central.

## The story in short

A boy full of regrets falls asleep in a classroom at HAU and wakes up in June of his third
year, five months before November 11, the day he lost a girl to the depression nobody
noticed. He has one more chance to save her, but every hour he gives her is an hour he
takes from his closest friend, the girl who, in his future, became his wife.

- **The heart of it:** a second chance with only his memories and one photo from his
  future, and a friend who knowingly steps back.
- **Tone:** quiet, nostalgic, sad and hopeful; told like the manga opening.
- **Language:** Tagalog and Taglish, with a little Kapampangan.
- **The game's question:** can he save her without losing everyone else?

Lance, Pat and Ysha are about 20, third-year students at HAU in 2026. The baby's name is
still to be decided.

## The rules

- **It isn't time travel; he is pulled into his past life.** He falls asleep in the empty
  classroom in 2038 and wakes up living his own life at 20, in June 2026, with all his
  adult memories. Nobody else knows.
- **Only his memories, and the photo.** There are no notes. The only thing that comes back
  with him is his wallet, and in it the family photo. In the game his memories are the
  quest log.
- **The family photo.** In the hospital on November 11, 2036, he took a selfie: him beside
  Pat in the bed, their newborn in her arms. Weeks later she printed it, wrote the baby's
  name and the date on the back, signed it, and slipped it into his wallet without a word.
  She has no idea what it will do. The date of the tragedy is also their child's birthday,
  ten years later.
- **November 11 is never shown.** Only the date and what it left behind.
- **Choices are remembered,** through the NPC memory the game already has.
- **The photo fades.** The more he chooses Ysha over Pat, the more it changes: first Pat's
  face blurs, then the baby. Being honest with Pat brings them back. The player can check it
  from the menu.

## How he is sent back

Nobody sends him on purpose, and nobody ever explains it.

1. **November 11, 2038.** Their child turns two. Alone in the car outside the party, he
   finally plays Ysha's voicemail from twelve years ago. Then he drives to HAU instead of
   going home, for the first time in years.
2. **The classroom.** Empty for the long weekend. His old third-year classroom, the window
   seat, the same afternoon light.
3. **The photo.** He looks at it: the best and the worst day of his life, ten years apart.
4. **"Sana..."** He thinks of every "sana" and falls asleep at the desk.
5. **"Huy. Gising na. Late ka na naman."** He wakes in June 2026 in the same seat, Pat
   shaking him awake. The wallet is in his pocket, the photo still inside.

**The way back.** On November 11, 2026 he feels the same pull to the same seat. If he falls
asleep there, he wakes in 2038 (endings 1 to 3). If he refuses, he stays in 2026 (ending 4).

His wife never knows. To her it was just an afternoon he came home late.

## Pat's story

- **Growing up.** The same street, the same schools, the same jeep home. She has loved him
  for years and never said it.
- **June.** He is different: older, quieter, and always near Ysha.
- **August: the photo.** He drops his wallet in the canteen. The photo falls out: a hospital
  room, him older, and her, holding a baby. On the back, her own signature, only older, the
  baby's name, and "November 11, 2036". For the first time she knows he could love her back.
- **The choice.** She decides not to hold him back, and starts quietly looking after Ysha
  too, without telling him.
- **Sa likod (September).** She sees them under the walkway. The song comes in on her face,
  and with it the future she just learned about. She leaves the drink on the fountain
  ledge and walks away.
- **The true ending.** She gives back the photo, turned to the signature: "Pirma ko 'yan.
  Alam ko na noon pa."

## Ysha's story

No single terrible thing happened to Ysha. A lot of ordinary heavy things piled up,
quietly, and nobody was close enough to see the pile.

- **The eldest, and the bright one.** Panganay, the first to go to college: "Ikaw ang
  pag-asa ng pamilya." She learned early that her job was to be fine.
- **First year:** a scholarship, a block of friends, good grades.
- **Second year:** her father skips work to drive her to an exam in a storm, and is fired
  for it. Nobody blames her; she blames herself. Her mother's health gets worse. She works
  nights at a milk tea shop and sleeps four hours a night.
- **She falls behind,** loses the scholarship, and her block moves on without her. She
  becomes irregular. At home she says school is fine; at school she says home is fine.
- **"Sayang."** A relative's word about her, with her in the room.
- **Third year (2026):** she stops eating lunch, posts happy photos, replies "haha" to
  everything, stops making plans past next week.

### The weight she carried in 2026

1. **"Ako ang dahilan."** Her father's firing. It mirrors Lance's guilt.
2. **Lola (July).** The only one who still asked "Kumain ka na ba, apo?" dies. After that,
   nobody asks. She plays her lola's last voice message on the jeep home.
3. **The rumour (September).** A joke about "the irreg girl who failed" in a big HAU group
   chat. She replies "haha grabe kayo". In the game the rumour spreads through the NPCs.
4. **The letter (November 10).** She will be dropped at the end of the semester. She tells
   no one. That night: "Lance, may sasabihin sana ako bukas. Okay lang ba?"

In the true ending none of these is fixed by Lance; she just doesn't carry them alone.

## What happened on November 11, 2026

**Trigger warning:** suicide, depression, guilt. Nothing is shown.

The truth Lance has buried for twelve years; the player learns it late, in fragments.

1. **Her birthday.** Ysha turned 21. "Walang babati sa'kin, promise." Nobody did, Lance
   included.
2. **The afternoon.** "Lance... pwede ba tayong mag-usap mamaya?" He snaps: "Ikaw na naman.
   Ang drama mo, Ysha." She laughs: "Joke lang. Sige, bukas na lang."
3. **The night that starts his happiness.** Pat asks him out; their first date, at the
   canteen. At 11:11 PM Ysha calls. He declines: "Bukas na lang."
4. **The voicemail.** He doesn't listen to it.
5. **The next morning.** Her seat is empty. That is all the story ever shows.
6. **The lie.** He says he hadn't talked to her that day and deletes the call. Pat never
   learns. Their whole life together began on the night Ysha was alone.
7. **The birthday he can't celebrate.** His child is born on November 11.
8. **The voicemail, twelve years later:**

> "Uy... sorry sa kanina. Birthday ko pala ngayon, haha. Wala lang... gusto ko lang marinig
> boses mo. Sige, bukas na lang. Ingat ka, Lance."

### How the trauma is shown

The weight comes from memory, aftermath and guilt, never from showing how she died.

- **The memory distorts:** the classroom stretches, the lamps flicker, her voice repeats
  wrong; 11:11 appears on clocks and jeep plates.
- **The aftermath:** her bag on the chair for a week; the group chat asking "Ysha, nasaan
  ka?"; her mother folding her uniform at the wake; her sibling: "Kuya, kaibigan ka ba ni
  Ate?"; her phone with one call to him that never connected.
- **The player is complicit:** early on, her call comes and the only button is "Decline".
- **The guilt rots him:** he can't hold his child on November 11; he flinches at phones at
  night.
- **The death stays unseen:** one black screen, a phone buzzing on a desk. Then morning.

### What the story must still say

No single person, and no single call, decides someone's life. Lance is responsible for
answering this time, not for everything before; someone says it to his face. Every release
opens with a content warning and ends with help information (in the Philippines, the NCMH
crisis line 1553; check current numbers before release).

## Key scenes

**Sa likod (September).** At dusk on the boulevard, Ysha: "Pagod na ako." Lance: "Nandito
lang ako." The camera turns: Pat by the fountain with two drinks. "Totoong Tayo" by Jin DC
comes in on her face, with a montage of the future she has never lived (first date, an
umbrella in the rain, graduation, the apartment, the wedding, the hospital room). She
leaves one drink on the ledge and walks away. The song needs permission before release;
use a placeholder until then.

## Endings

| # | Ending | How he gets there | Where he ends up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Saved, but alone | Gives everything to Ysha, pushes Pat away, still means to go home | 2038: Ysha alive; his wife and child never existed |
| 2 | The same November 11 | Stays close to Pat, misses Ysha's signs again | 2038: his family, and the regret twice as heavy |
| 3 | Hindi ka nag-iisa (true) | Tells Pat the truth; Ysha gets real help | 2038: his family, Ysha alive as the child's ninang |
| 4 | Ang Pinili Ko (hidden) | Saves Ysha, falls for her, never sleeps in the classroom | Stays in 2026 for good, with Ysha |

- **Ending 1.** Nobody dies; the family simply never happened. The photo shows him alone
  by an empty bed. At an alumni event Pat, happy with someone else, greets him: "Uy! Ikaw
  pala. Kumusta ka na?" Every November 11 Ysha messages: "Salamat pa rin."
- **Ending 2.** He keeps Ysha at a distance, thinking there is time. November 11 comes
  again. In 2038, at the birthday, his wife (who knows nothing) asks: "Iniisip mo na naman
  siya?" "Hindi mo kasalanan." He doesn't believe her. The last shot is the classroom.
- **Ending 3.** In October he tells Pat everything: "Sige. Tutulungan kita." They sit with
  Ysha, walk her to the jeep, go with her to the guidance office and to her family. On
  November 11 she is with them, under one umbrella too small for three. At the fountain Pat
  hands back the photo: "Pirma ko 'yan. Alam ko na noon pa... Kasi gusto kong mabuhay siya.
  At kung pipiliin mo ako, gusto ko dahil gusto mo, hindi dahil sa litrato." In 2038: "Late
  ka na naman," and a group selfie where everyone is looking at the camera.
- **Ending 4.** He leaves the fading photo on the classroom desk and walks out before he can
  fall asleep. Pat finds it, runs to the fountain, sees them in love, and watches the photo
  fade in her hand until only he is left. Years with Ysha; November 11 still unsettles him;
  the last panel is his wallet with an empty slot.

In every ending Ysha gets real help; her recovery is never "fixed by love".
