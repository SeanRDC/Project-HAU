# Campus Layout

How the real Holy Angel University campus is laid out in the game, where the numbers come from, and how to correct them.

The goal is a one-to-one copy of the campus: every building in its real place, real distances between them, at one scale. This document describes the first pass. It was built from four sources and is only as good as they are. More photos will make it better.

## Sources

**Since 2026-10-12 the buildings come from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/16198709)** (OSM): the real, traced outline of 19 of the 21 numbered buildings, the campus boundary, every street and path around it, and the neighbouring buildings for the map. `npm run import:osm` downloads them again into `src/game/campus/osm.ts`; run it whenever someone improves HAU on openstreetmap.org. OSM's licence (ODbL) asks for the credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors", which the C map shows.

Lined up against OSM, the aerial-photo plan had the scale right (to 0.6%) and most buildings within 5 m, but some were 20 to 30 m off (St. Raphael, St. Michael, MGN, the Gym Annex) and many were drawn too big. Three junction points had landed inside APS, the covered court and the gym.

The aerial photo is still the source for the gates, the junctions, GGN (10) and the Formation Center (16). OSM still shows the old Sta. Clara buildings where GGN stands now; those are left out.

Plus one measurement taken in Google Earth on 2026-10-11: **140.36 m from the rotunda statue to the nose of the chapel.** That single distance sets the scale for everything.

## The anchor: the chapel

The centre of the Chapel of the Holy Guardian Angel is (0, 0). Every other position is in metres from it.

Positions do not use north and east. The campus is built on a grid turned about 31 degrees from north, and the buildings line up with that grid, so the plan uses it too:

* **u** runs along the chapel walkway. Positive is towards the chapel and beyond it; the main gate is at about u = -274.
* **v** runs across the walkway. Positive is to your right as you walk to the chapel (the Main Building side); negative is the PGN side.

The walkway direction read off the photo was 2.64 degrees off the true grid; OSM's building edges showed it, so the plan is turned by that much (`GRID_TURN` in `plan.ts`). The scale works out to **0.454 m per aerial pixel**. In the scenes, **9 game pixels are one metre**. That was chosen because the DJN Building and the angel pedestals, drawn before any of this, already fit it.

## Where it lives in the code

* `src/game/campus/osm.ts` is generated by `tools/import-osm.mjs`: building outlines, roads, the campus boundary, already in plan metres. Do not edit it by hand. The tool says which OSM way is which building.
* `src/game/campus/plan.ts` holds the data: each building's blocks, the gates, the junctions. A building with an OSM outline takes its blocks from it (the box round it, or parts read off it for the L-shaped ones). Gates and junctions are written as aerial pixels, or as metres where they were moved onto an OSM path.
* `src/game/campus/streets.ts` turns the plan into scenes. Each outdoor scene is one street, a line on the plan, plus the direction the camera looks. `xOf` and `frontage` say where a place or a building falls along that line.
* Each scene in `src/game/scenes/` asks those functions where to draw things. Nothing is typed in by hand.

So to move a building: fix it on openstreetmap.org and run `npm run import:osm`, or for the ones OSM does not have, read the new pixel off the aerial photo and change it in `plan.ts`. The scenes and the map follow.

Press **C** in the game for the campus map. It paints the real OSM outlines, so it is the quickest check of the plan.

## What is still a guess

* **GGN (10) and the Formation Center (16).** Not in OSM. The Formation Center as placed overlaps the chapel; it may be attached to it, or it may be the small unnamed building OSM has about 25 m away.
* **Building 1** is matched to an unnamed OSM building by size and place.
* **The scenes still use blocks.** The map draws the real outlines, but the street scenes lay buildings out from rectangles: the box round each outline, or two or three parts for the L-shaped ones.
* **What every building looks like**, apart from the DJN and its courtyard, the Epiphany, the rotunda fountain, the walkway, St. Joseph Hall, SFJ and the gatehouse, which were drawn from photos. The others are drawn from five generic facade styles with colours and floor counts taken from the campus map drawing. Floor counts in particular are guesses.
* **SJH and SFJ are squeezed.** A real storey is about 3.5 m, 31 pixels at this scale, and eight of them would not fit on the screen, so their floors are 20 to 24 pixels. The floor counts (SJH: seven on the left wing, eight on the right; SFJ: five, with the glass bay and the pylon taller) were counted off one photo each.
* **SFJ is wider than its plot.** The front in the photo is drawn across about 38 m so that its doors sit straight behind the pool, as they do; the plan gives that side 31 m. The scene got 9 m longer at that end to hold it.
* **Where the side gate is.** Sean says the gate students use is a side gate near the Main Building, so the gate scene now sits at the plan's `barachielGate` spot (the gate beside the Main Building, read off the aerial photo as the vehicle gate on Holy Angel Avenue) and opens straight on to the plaza. Whether the side gate is that gate or another one close by, and what street it is on, has not been checked. The gate at the street end of the Epiphany is now a closed gate you can only look at.
* **Which side of each building the door is on.**
* **The courtyard gate** of the Main Building. The game puts it on the walkway side; this has not been checked.
* **The junction points** where roads meet. Most were read off the aerial; four (`hallsCorner`, `northJunction`, `northEastJunction`, `gymCorner`) were moved onto OSM paths. The game's street network is still simpler than the real one.
* **Gate names.** The gate-area diagram names Sto. Rosario Gate (main), St. Barachiel Gate (vehicles), Guardian Angel Gate, a courtyard/pedestrian gate, St. Raphael Gate and St. Uriel Gate. Only the first two are in the game. The guard house at St. Barachiel Gate still says "GATE 1" from before.
* **The diagram calls building 1 "Plaza de Amor"** where the campus map says "Plaza de Corazon". The game uses the campus map's name.

## The two older scenes that are not to scale

`plaza` and `gate` were drawn before the plan existed. They keep their own spacing so their cutscenes and dialogue positions still work. `streets.ts` ties each to the plan by its two ends (see `FIXED`), which is enough for the map to show where you are. Bringing them to scale is a job for later; the plaza is about twice as long as the real road in front of the Main Building.

## Interiors

There are none beyond the DJN lobby and courtyard, on purpose. Each building's door tells you its name, its number on the campus map and what offices are inside, and that it is not open yet. Interiors wait for reference photos of the inside of each building.

## What would help most next

1. **Add GGN and the Formation Center to OpenStreetMap** (anyone with a free account can trace them on openstreetmap.org), then run `npm run import:osm`.
2. A photo of the front of each building, taken from the road, so the generic facades can be replaced.
3. A photo from the rotunda looking down each of its roads.
4. Where the doors are.

## Reference images

The pictures in `assets/` came from Google Earth, a Google Maps photo sphere, the university's own campus map and photos found online. They were kept out of the repository at first because they are other people's pictures and this repository is public. On 2026-10-12 Sean decided to commit them so the whole team has the same references. Keep them under the same file names so the notes in `plan.ts` and in the art files still point at the right picture. They are references only: nothing in the game loads them, and they should not go into a build. If anyone who owns one of them asks, take it out.
